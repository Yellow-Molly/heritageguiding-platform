# Brief 01 — Tour Content (Copywriter)

**Recipient:** Tour copywriter (heritage/travel domain)
**Deadline:** TBD by project lead (set per locale in kickoff)
**Format:** Notion doc per tour, one tab per locale (SV / EN / DE)
**Submit to:** Project lead → CMS editor
**Contact:** Project lead

---

## Context

We are seeding a Payload CMS at MVP launch. Five Stockholm heritage tours need original copy in three locales (Swedish, English, German). All fields below map 1:1 to Payload `Tours` collection — please respect char limits exactly.

---

## Deliverables

- **5 tours × 3 locales = 15 content sets**
- Each set delivered as a structured Notion doc per tour with three locale tabs
- All copy original (no AI slop, no scraped competitor text)

## Specifications per field

| Field | Char limit | Locales | Example |
|-------|-----------|---------|---------|
| `title` | ≤200 | SV/EN/DE | "Gamla Stan – En vandring i historiens hjärta" |
| `shortDescription` | ≤160 | SV/EN/DE | One-sentence hook for cards/meta |
| `description` | 500–1500 (rich text) | SV/EN/DE | Full prose; 2–4 paragraphs |
| `highlights` | 1–10 bullets, ≤80 chars each | SV/EN/DE | "Inträde till Stortorgets museum" |
| `accessibility.notes` | ≤300 | SV/EN/DE | Per-impairment notes (mobility/hearing/visual) |
| `metaDescription` | ≤160 | SV/EN/DE | SEO snippet (may differ from shortDescription) |

### Tone
- Heritage-focused, expert but warm
- No superlatives without basis ("legendary", "must-see" → cut)
- Sweden-specific cultural context welcome
- Konsumentverket-truthful (factual, no aspirational claims)

### Reference tours (copy style only — do NOT copy text)
- Visit competitor sites for style; project lead will share 2 references in kickoff

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All 5 tours delivered, all 3 locales each
- [ ] Char limits respected per field (use word counter)
- [ ] Highlights are sentence-cased, no period at end
- [ ] No "TBD", "Coming soon", placeholder text
- [ ] Accessibility notes specific (not generic "wheelchair-friendly")
- [ ] Native-speaker review per locale (mark with reviewer initials in doc)

## How to submit

- Notion doc shared with project lead — per-locale tab, per-field section
- Final approval before CMS seeding

## Questions / contact

- Brand voice questions → marketing lead
- Field semantics → project lead
- Accessibility wording → a11y reviewer (phase 08 brief)
